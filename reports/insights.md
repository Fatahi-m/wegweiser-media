# Insights for the topic agent – week from 23.11.2026 (from weekly report 22.11.2026)

1. **"Money you don't have to pay / Jobcenter pays" is the strongest evergreen type.** Rundfunkbeitrag (fa 12.4K / ku 10.8K) and Nachzahlung (fa 13.6K, IG ku 1.6K = best IG reel ever) had the highest save rates (2.0–2.2 %). Plan 2 per page this week, e.g. Erstausstattung/Waschmaschine from Jobcenter, Mehrbedarf Warmwasser, Bildungspaket (school trips, Nachhilfe), Wohngeld application before 31.12 (⚠️ bill, not law).
2. **News with a hard date wins when posted 1–3 days before the date** (Klarna 20.11: best video on both TikToks, 22.8K / 16.4K, share rate ku 3.2 %). Next candidates: what changes on 01.01.2027 per topic (Minijob 633 €, Mindestlohn 14.60 €, Deutschlandticket 66.80 €), Jobcenter January payment ~30.12 – publish mid/late December, not too early.
3. **Cut length to 60–70 s.** This week every video was 73–92 s and the series part 25-4-5 (117 s) was the weakest IG reel (41 plays). Shorter = one message, 3 points max, details "in the web app".
4. **Residence series: favour broad "which one are you?" overviews over narrow paragraphs.** 25 Abs. 4/5 (IG fa 41 plays) and 4a/4b (ku 4.8K, 0.4 % shares) were weak; the overview کدوم ۲۵ got 34.1K / 15.3K. Next residence video should be a broad identity question (e.g. "Gestattung, Duldung or Aufenthaltserlaubnis – what's on your paper?") rather than another niche §.
5. **Skip topics for people outside Germany** (Studentenvisum 3.2K / 3.3K, bottom on both pages). Our audience already lives here.
6. **Kurdish audience reacts to prices/cost-of-living** (Strom/Benzin ku 8.7K, 9.2 % likes, 45 comments vs fa 2.0K). Use easy personal comment questions with a number ("how much do you pay per month?") – they produce comments.
7. **One TikTok per page per day is enough;** a second post the same day did not add reach (2 of 3 cases weaker). Kurdish Nachzahlung got only 541 TikTok views while IG ku and fa did well → check TikTok Studio for a restriction before re-using that format; re-upload only after 2–3 days with a new caption.
8. **Likes are fine** – like rate rose to fa ≈4.9 % / ku ≈6.3 % (from 3.1 % / 4.7 %). The issue is reach (median views −25–30 %); work on the first-2-seconds hook (money + "you" + date), not on asking for likes.
